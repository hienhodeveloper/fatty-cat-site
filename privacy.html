Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Fatty Cat does not collect, store or share any personal data.

No account and no sign in are needed to play. Your best score, chosen cat, sound and vibration settings are saved only on this device and are removed when you delete the app.

The game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third party services. It never asks for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Fatty Cat runs fully offline and sends nothing to the internet.

The game is safe for players of every age and contains no user generated content, no chat and no purchases.

Questions about privacy? Write to hienhodeveloper@gmail.com and we answer within a few days.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third party scripts, and its fonts are served from this site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement.
