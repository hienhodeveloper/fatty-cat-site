Common questions

How do I play?

Press and hold anywhere on the screen to grow the stick. Let go and it falls across the gap. If it reaches the next pillar, your cat walks over and you score a point. Too short or too long, and the cat falls.

What is Perfect +2?

Every pillar has a small red mark in the middle. Drop the stick right on it and you score 2 points instead of 1.

How do I change my cat?

On the start screen, tap Cats, choose any of the 31 cats, then tap Done. Every cat is free.

Can I keep the sky in one colour?

The sky follows your clock by default. Open Settings and tap Sky to switch between Auto, Morning, Noon and Night.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Open Settings from the start screen and switch Sound or Vibration off. During a game you can also tap pause, then Sound Off.

Where is my best score saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the app removes your best score and chosen cat, and they don't move to a new device.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Fatty Cat is free, with no ads, no purchases and no account to create, so there is nothing to restore.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game works fully offline and never connects to the internet.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.
